The Jura Sanctuary

Ancestral Craftsmanship

Three unbroken disciplines observed at our high watchmaking atelier in Switzerland.

I 3 Days / Dial

Le Guillochage Main

Sacred Dial Turning

Each dial is individually engraved using hand-cranked straight-line and rose engine machines dating to 1895. The artisan guides diamond-tipped chisels with fingertip sensitivity to slice shimmering wave patterns.

II 40 Hours / Bridge

L'Anglage & Polissage

Internal Sharp Angles

Internal right angles cannot be finished by robotic CNC tools. Our artisans bevel each titanium and German silver bridge with diamond paste and elderberry pegs to produce luminous specular black mirror polishes.

III Fairmined Gold

Métallurgie Noble

Proprietary Alloys

We smelt our proprietary Honey Rose Gold in-house, balancing pure 24K gold with precise measurements of copper and palladium to ensure zero oxidation or patina fade across multiple generations of wear.
